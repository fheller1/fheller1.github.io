## About Me

`printf("%s", "Hello world!");`
I am Frederik, a final-year master’s student in Computational Engineering at Technische Universität Darmstadt, where I received my bachelor’s degree in 2024. During both my bachelor’s and master’s studies, I was fortunate to work with [Alap Kshirsagar](https://alapkshirsagar.github.io/) at the [Intelligent Autonomous Systems (IAS) Lab](https://www.ias.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/Main/IAS), led by [Jan Peters](https://www.ias.informatik.tu-darmstadt.de/Team/JanPeters). Most recently, I had a wonderful time visiting the [Tsinghua Embodied AI Lab](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/Research/Research_Groups/Tsinghua_Embodied_AI_Lab.htm) led by [Huazhe Xu](https://hxu.rocks/) at Tsinghua University.

My research interests lie in learning for robotic manipulation, spanning policy learning, dynamics modeling, and dexterous manipulation, with a focus on tactile perception.

I have also spent half a year interning at Porsche AG in Weissach, exploring machine learning for vehicle safety, and worked part-time in software engineering for two years.

**I am actively looking for 2027 PhD opportunities.** Feel free to explore this portfolio or reach out to me!
